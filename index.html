About

As Manager of Insights & Analytics on Tyson's Walmart business, I own the enterprise analytics platform that turns raw supplier feeds into fast, trustworthy reporting for the whole account, spanning sales, inventory, replenishment, purchase orders, fill rate, OTIF, margin and claims, and transportation planning.

What I do spans the full analytics stack, from the raw data to the decision. On the engineering side, I design the BigQuery models and pipelines that make enterprise-scale data fast and reliable, and I tune the ones that are not. But the engineering is in service of the harder part: turning that data into something a business team can actually use. I build flexible, self-serve reports people can configure themselves, translate messy operational reality into clear guidance, and partner across sales, replenishment, transportation, customer service, and technical teams to answer questions that cross all of them.

The thread through all of it is trust. A fast model that is wrong is worse than useless, so I verify before I believe, and I would rather deliver a smaller answer I can stand behind than a bigger one I cannot.

Before moving into analytics leadership, I spent three years in Sales Strategy and Planning, owning analytics for major branded prepared-foods portfolios and partnering closely with demand planning. That commercial grounding keeps my data work tied to outcomes rather than just outputs. I am completing my M.S. in Data Science while doing this work, which keeps theory and practice in constant conversation.

Featured · Time Series

Are American Wages Actually Keeping Up?

A time series analysis of inflation, wage growth, and consumer spending from 2000 to 2026 using Federal Reserve data. Real wages are up on net, but the gains were interrupted exactly when households felt the most strain. The centerpiece is a cautionary finding: a statistically significant regression that turned out to be a pandemic composition artifact and vanished under a robustness check, a reminder that significance is not truth.
